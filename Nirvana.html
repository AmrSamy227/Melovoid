About

#140 in the world

Nirvana was an American rock band formed in Aberdeen, Washington, in 1987. Founded by lead singer and guitarist Kurt Cobain and bassist Krist Novoselic, the band went through a succession of drummers, most notably Chad Channing, before recruiting Dave Grohl in 1990. Nirvana's breakthrough came with the 1991 album "Nevermind" and its iconic single "Smells Like Teen Spirit," which brought alternative rock into the mainstream. The band's raw sound and emotionally charged lyrics defined the grunge movement and influenced countless artists. Despite their brief career, ending with Cobain's death in 1994, Nirvana remains one of the most influential and important rock bands in history.
