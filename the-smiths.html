About

#343 in the world

The Smiths were an English rock band formed in Manchester in 1982. The group consisted of vocalist Morrissey, guitarist Johnny Marr, bassist Andy Rourke, and drummer Mike Joyce. Renowned for their jangly guitar sound and Morrissey's distinctive baritone voice and literate lyrics, The Smiths are considered one of the most important acts to emerge from the British independent music scene of the 1980s. Their influence on alternative rock continues to resonate decades after their breakup in 1987.
